# ezrsurvey: Single-Line Helpers for Consumer Survey Analysis

Simplifies the day-to-day work of research and insights professionals
who analyse consumer and customer survey data but have limited coding
experience. Wraps common 'tidyverse' patterns into single-line functions
for the tasks these teams repeat constantly: loading and combining
survey exports, turning questions into percentages and summaries,
recoding rating and Net Promoter scales, deriving demographics such as
age cohorts and regions, modelling the drivers of satisfaction, checking
sampling precision, selecting illustrative open-text comments, and
producing presentation-ready charts and reports. A simulated
consumer-survey dataset is included for learning and examples.
